## How to cite

The article describing the method is in preparation. Meanwhile, please cite the software:

> Pareja Gutiérrez, J. A., and Gamarra Acosta, M. R. (2026). *RabMV: Registry-Aware Boosting Machine of
> Valledupar* (version 0.1.0) [Computer software]. https://github.com/jannerpareja/rabmv

```bibtex
@software{rabmv,
  author  = {Pareja Gutiérrez, Janer Alfonso and Gamarra Acosta, Margarita Rosa},
  title   = {{RabMV: Registry-Aware Boosting Machine of Valledupar}},
  version = {0.1.0},
  year    = {2026},
  url     = {https://github.com/jannerpareja/rabmv},
  license = {BSD-3-Clause}
}
```

The repository's `CITATION.cff` holds the same information, and GitHub shows it under «Cite this repository».

## Acknowledgements

We thank the **Secretaría de Salud Municipal de Valledupar** (Cesar, Colombia), whose surveillance registry of
suicide attempts and completed suicides motivated the method and was used in its development. The method is
named after the city for this reason.

The registry data are used under the terms agreed with the Secretaría. They are not distributed with this
software, and no individual record appears in the repository: every output derived from them is an aggregate,
with counts from 1 to 4 suppressed. All data in the repository are synthetic.
